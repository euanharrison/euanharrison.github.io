RENOVATION 04 / 08

Rear Window Restoration

Window refurbishment and weather sealing

10 hours

Work completed

  • Removed the windows from their frame.
  • Scraped, sanded, primed, and painted the windows.
  • Secured the glass panes using linseed-oil putty.
  • Repaired the exterior frame with wood filler and all-weather sealant.
  • Painted the rear wall and power-washed the windowsill.
RENOVATION 05 / 08

Double-Window Panelling

Timber framing, mouldings and finishing

35 hours

Work completed

  • Removed the old panelling and built a supporting frame.
  • Attached front and side panels, mouldings, and frontal panelling.
  • Fitted the windowsill and beading.
  • Removed and planed the shutter to the correct height.
  • Primed and painted, then repeated the process on the other side.
RENOVATION 06 / 08

Courtyard Door

Door hardware and draught-proofing

5 hours

Work completed

  • Removed the old handle and filled previous screw holes.
  • Purchased and installed a replacement handle.
  • Installed a new draught liner and repositioned the deadlocks.
  • Primed and painted the interior and exterior of both doors.

2 hours

RENOVATION 08 / 08

Living Room Renovation

Floor restoration and interior decorating

38 hours

Work completed

  • Cleared furniture, removed and cut up the existing carpet.
  • Removed carpet grippers and more than 100 staples (3 hours).
  • Sanded the timber floor (20 hours).
  • Painted interior frames and panelling (1 hour).
  • Covered water stains with stain-blocking primer (1 hour), and painted the ceiling white (2 hours).
  • Removed wallpaper beside the window, sanded the wood, and plastered gaps (3 hours).
  • Painted the upper walls white (1 hour) and the walls olive-tree green (3 hours).
  • Vacuumed, mopped and dusted thoroughly (1 hour).
